Professional Profile

A Life of
Leadership & Governance

CA Mohanlalji Bhandari is a qualified Chartered Accountant with decades of deep expertise spanning finance, organizational consulting, institutional banking, and executive business leadership. Through a long and distinguished professional journey, he has anchored multiple boards, strategic panels, and corporate stewardship roles across various growth sectors.

Known for his sharp adherence to ethical compliance, strategic planning, and commercial insight, he has served on the boards of premier institutions including RBL Bank and Everest Industries Ltd., shaping business policies and robust financial structures.

His professional career is paired with a lasting dedication to mentoring young professionals and building organizational institutions that stand the test of time.

The Institution

The MGB Legacy
of Excellence

CA Mohanlalji Bhandari founded MGB Group in 1979 as an independent, practicing accountancy firm. Through a relentless commitment to rigorous practice and business transparency, the practice has matured into a multi-disciplinary, full-service advisory powerhouse with over 350 skilled team members.

Operating across six strategic corporate hubs in India alongside a dedicated office in GIFT City Dubai, the firm serves global organizations with a complete stack of financial advisory solutions built upon a solid base of trust and integrity.
